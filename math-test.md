## Algebra

The quadratic formula gives the roots of $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ as $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$, where the discriminant $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$ tells you how many real roots there are.

## Geometry

A circle of radius $r$ has area $A = \pi r^2$ and circumference $C = 2\pi r$. For a right triangle with legs $a$ and $b$, the hypotenuse is $c = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$.

## Calculus

The derivative of $f(x) = x^n$ is $f'(x) = n x^{n-1}$, and the integral $\int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3}$. Euler's identity, $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$, links five fundamental constants.

## Series

The geometric series $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} r^k = \frac{1}{1-r}$ converges when $|r| < 1$, and the Basel problem gives $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}$.

## Linear Algebra

A matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ is invertible iff $\det(A) \neq 0$. An eigenvector $\mathbf{v}$ satisfies $A\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}$ for some scalar $\lambda$.

## Probability

For a normal distribution $X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$, about 68% of values fall in $[\mu - \sigma, \mu + \sigma]$. Bayes' theorem states $P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(B \mid A)\,P(A)}{P(B)}$.
